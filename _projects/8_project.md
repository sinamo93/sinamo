This project develops a computationally efficient, data-driven algorithm to identify active feeder topologies in distribution networks using purely smart meter voltage magnitude measurements without requiring complete phase angle observability.

**Publication:**
* **S. Mohammadi**, V. Bui, W. Su (2025). "An Efficient Voltage-Based Topology Recovery Method for Distribution Networks," *57th North American Power Symposium (NAPS)*, Hartford, CT, USA. [DOI: 10.1109/NAPS66256.2025.11272327](https://doi.org/10.1109/NAPS66256.2025.11272327)
