While black-box neural networks can estimate fault current phasors rapidly, protective relays require interpretable, computationally lightweight mathematical expressions for real-time hardware deployment.

Supported by the UM-Dearborn EXP+ Independent Research Grant, **SymbolicPhasor** leverages Deep Symbolic Regression (DSR) to discover closed-form analytical equations for accurate phasor estimation under fault transients.

**Publication:**
* **S. Mohammadi**, W. Su (2026). "SymbolicPhasor: Power System Phasor Estimation via Deep Symbolic Regression," *58th North American Power Symposium (NAPS)* (Accepted). [arXiv:2608.08552](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.08552)
