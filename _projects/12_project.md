This project develops a transformer-guided variable-projection framework for jointly estimating power-system phasors and fault inception time under decaying DC offsets.

**Publication:**

- M. C. Wada, **S. Mohammadi**, V. Hai-Bui, W. Su (2026). "Transformer-guided Variable Projection for Phasor and Fault-Time Estimation Under DDC Offset," _2027 IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM)_ (Submitted). [arXiv:2611.03416](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.03416)
