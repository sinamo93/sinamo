The rapid expansion of Artificial Intelligence (AI) computing infrastructure imposes massive, highly dynamic, sub-second load fluctuations on utility grids.

This work provides a comprehensive framework and critical evaluation of multi-layer energy storage systems (ESS)—spanning chip-level buffering, rack-level storage, grid-interactive UPS (GiUPS), and grid-scale battery and second-life storage—to mitigate oscillation risks and provide active frequency and voltage support.

**Publication:**

- **S. Mohammadi**, W. Wang, M. Wada, R. Haghighi, A. Hassan, H. Liu, A. Bhatnagar, A. Chen, W. Su (2026). "Grid Integration of AI Data Centers: A Critical Review of Energy Storage Solutions," _Advances in Applied Energy_, 100291. [DOI: 10.1016/j.adapen.2026.100291](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adapen.2026.100291)
