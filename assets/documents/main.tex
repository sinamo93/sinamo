\section{~~Research Experience}

~~\textbf{Graduate Student Research Assistant, Department of Electrical \& Computer Engineering,\\
~~University of Michigan-Dearborn, Aug 2023 - now}\\
~~Working on topology estimation of low voltage distribution networks.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Two novel methods proposed for topology recovery in low voltage distribution networks using only smart meter data.  
    \item Hierarchical clustering of incremental voltage measurements can reveal the topology of distribution network with high accuracy.
    \item Investigating physics-informed neural networks for solving optimal power flow.   
    \item A label-free neural network is trained for DC optimal power flow with high accuracy comparing to ground truth obtained via analytical solution.
\end{itemize}

~~\textbf{Graduate MSc student, Department of Electrical Engineering, University of Zanjan, 2021}\\
~~Proposing a novel method for protecting meshed microgrids.
\begin{itemize}
    \item A pilot based unit protection method is proposed based on apparent resistance estimation of transmission lines.  
    \item The efficiency of method is evaluated for meshed microgrids in both grid-connected and islanded modes under different contingencies. 
\end{itemize}
